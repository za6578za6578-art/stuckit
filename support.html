If the watch records nothing

  1. Install Stuckit on your Apple Watch: open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and install it.
  2. Open Stuckit on the watch once and allow the workout. The workout keeps it recording when your arm drops at address.
  3. Wear the watch on your lead wrist, the one nearer the target, with the crown toward your fingers.
  4. Tap the watch before each shot and stand still until it buzzes.

If calibration says two positions came out backwards

One of those two sets went to the wrong length. Hit both again, making each clearly longer than the last. Stuckit keeps your other position.
